1 Executive Summary and Target Outcome
The ERP shall be a single integrated platform for the company's complete operational and financial lifecycle. It shall supportone legal entity with multiple branches, formal departments, multiple warehouses, multiple bank accounts, independent costcentres and consolidated company reporting.
Governing authority
Issa Mohammed is the sole Business Process Owner. Department managers and employees provide operationalinformation, but the final business process, accounting treatment, permissions, workflow, document lifecycle,reporting design and implementation priority are approved only by Issa Mohammed.
1.1 Target outcome
One controlled source of truth for master data, source documents, approvals, operational movements, subledgers,journal entries and reports.Complete traceability from every posted accounting line to the originating document, branch, department, costcentre, business partner, item, warehouse, bank account, attachment and user action.No deletion of saved or posted records. Drafts may be cancelled and retained. Final documents may only becancelled or corrected through an approved reversing document or full reversal journal.Automatic posting from approved operational documents according to the approved posting matrix and configurableaccount mappings.English-only user interface and documents.Operational currencies at launch: IQD and USD, with additional currencies configurable. IQD is the primarytransaction and ledger currency; USD values are reporting equivalents calculated using approved historicalexchange rates.
1.2 Mandatory architecture
Subledgers, control accounts and dimensions
The system shall maintain detailed customer, supplier, inventory, fixed-asset, bank, project and service subledgersthat reconcile to the General Ledger. Branches, departments, cost centres, projects, warehouses and service linesshall be analytical dimensions. The Chart of Accounts shall remain hierarchical and configurable.
1.3 Required scope
The build includes Foundation and Security, Master Data, CRM, Sales, Purchasing, Inventory and Warehouses, Projects,Contracting, Logistics, Money Transfer, Investments, Core Financials, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Treasury,Fixed Assets, Budgeting, HR, Payroll, Employee Expenses and Advances, Document Management, Reporting, Integrations,Migration, Testing and Go-Live.
1.4 Accounting governance
Accounting recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure shall follow applicable IFRS Accounting Standards.System configuration shall support controlled future updates when accounting standards or approved company policieschange. Operational workflows and approval rules remain company-defined requirements.

3 Core Architecture and Integration Principles
3.1 One source of truth
Each master record and transaction shall have one authoritative record and a unique system identifier.Downstream documents shall use Copy From or controlled conversion rather than re-entry.The system shall prevent duplicate posting through transaction identifiers and idempotency controls.
3.2 Standard document lifecycle
The common lifecycle is Draft, Submitted for Approval where required, Approved, Executed/Posted, Partially Completed,Completed/Closed, Cancelled or Reversed. Each document type shall use only the states applicable to its operational andaccounting effect.A saved draft cannot be deleted; it may be edited or cancelled.A final operational or accounting document cannot be edited or deleted.A document with no accounting or inventory effect, such as an unexecuted Purchase Order, may be cancelledwithout a journal reversal.A posted document may be corrected only by the approved reversal or return document for that process.

3.3 Posting engine
Operational documents shall create accounting entries automatically when their approved lifecycle event requiresposting.All accounts used by automatic or manual journals shall exist and be active in the Chart of Accounts.Posting accounts shall be selected through configurable accounting mappings, not hard-coded account numbers.Every journal shall be balanced in IQD and retain source currency, historical rate and USD reporting equivalent.Each posting shall retain the source module, document and line identifiers for complete drill-down.
3.4 System-wide controls
Server-side permission enforcement.Department and branch data scope.Complete immutable audit trail.Unique document numbering.Soft-close accounting periods.No negative inventory.Document attachment and related-record links.Controlled data import with validation and error reporting.

5 Security, Users, Roles, Workflow and Audit
5.1 User and department assignment
Every user shall be assigned to one or more authorised departments, branches and data scopes. Super Users retain fulladministration access. Operational access shall be granted by screen and action rather than by menu visibility alone.
5.2 Department Manager control
The user record shall contain a Department Manager toggle for each assigned department.A user marked as Department Manager can create and finalise documents within that department without a secondapproval.A user who is not marked as Department Manager shall submit the document to the assigned Department Manager.

6 CRM and Customer Management
CRM is the commercial relationship module. It captures prospective customers, enquiries, opportunities, activities andcustomer history before a Sales Order or Project exists. It uses the same Business Partner record used by Sales, Finance,Projects, Logistics and Money Transfer.
Module menu and principal screens
CRM Dashboard; Leads and Enquiries; Opportunities; Activities and Calendar; Contacts; Business Partner Onboarding;Customer 360; Campaign and Lead Source; After-Sales and Warranty Cases; CRM Reports; CRM Settings.
End-to-end workflow
Create or import a lead and assign an owner.Qualify the lead and convert it to an opportunity.Create or approve the Business Partner record when commercial activity requires it.Record requested products, expected value, probability, activities and next action.Convert the approved opportunity directly into a Sales Order or Project, according to the business line.Retain traceability from lead to opportunity, order/project, invoice, receipt and after-sales case.
Core business rules and validations
Duplicate detection by name, phone, email, registration number and bank details.A lead can exist without an approved Business Partner; a Sales Order, Project, invoice or service transaction cannot.Business Partner status supports Prospect, Active, On Hold, Blocked and Inactive.Completed activities and stage changes remain in the audit trail.Customer 360 respects department, branch and record-level permission.
Integration and data relationships
Uses the central Business Partner master.Creates Sales Orders or Projects without duplicate data entry.

7 Sales and Order-to-Cash
Sales begins directly with a Sales Order. Price negotiation and external customer communication can occur through Exceloutside the ERP, after which the authorised user creates or pastes the item lines into the Sales Order.
7.1 Module menu and principal screens
Sales Dashboard; Customer Price Lists; Sales Orders; Stock Reservations; Pick Lists; Delivery Notes; A/R Invoices; CashSales; Customer Receipts; Credit Control; Sales Returns; Customer Credit Memos; Warranty Inquiry; Sales Reports; SalesSettings.
7.2 Approved workflow
External Excel -> Sales Order -> Automatic Stock Reservation -> Pick List -> Goods Issue / Delivery Note -> A/RInvoice on the same delivery date -> Customer Receipt.Partial deliveries and multiple deliveries from one Sales Order are supported.One Sales Order may contain lines for multiple branches, warehouses and delivery locations.Proof of Delivery shall capture recipient name, signature, attachments and delivery photos.The normal product-sale process contains product items only; installation, transport and other services are notincluded in this Sales Order workflow.
7.3 Pricing, discounts and credit control
Each Business Partner shall be linked to one designated Price List.Unit prices are retrieved from the customer's linked Price List and cannot be edited in the Sales Order.Discounts are allowed only at line level.Sales Orders created by ordinary Sales users require Sales Manager approval. Sales Orders created by theauthorised Sales Manager finalise directly.Customer Credit Limit, Payment Terms, Available Credit, Outstanding Balance, Overdue Balance and Open OrderExposure shall be calculated in real time.The system shall block approval when the Credit Limit is exceeded. Only the Sales Manager can override the block,and the reason is mandatory.
7.4 Inventory, invoice and revenue rules
Stock is reserved automatically when the Sales Order is approved.The system shall not approve a Sales Order when Available Stock is insufficient.Every inventory A/R Invoice shall be created from an approved Delivery Note.

One supplier per Purchase Order.The supplier must be an active Business Partner with Supplier role.Lines can be Inventory Item, Service, Fixed Asset or Expense.Item selection uses the internal item code. 9 Inventory and Warehouse Management
Inventory shall control quantity, availability, reservation, movement, serial/batch identity and FIFO cost across all branchesand warehouses.
9.1 Warehouse types
Main Warehouse; Branch Warehouse; Transit Warehouse; Quarantine Warehouse; Damaged Goods Warehouse; ReturnsWarehouse. A branch can contain multiple warehouses.
9.2 Valuation and stock controls
FIFO is the single valuation method for every item and warehouse.Negative inventory is prohibited without exception.Every inventory issue consumes the oldest available FIFO cost layers.A reversal restores the original quantity and cost relationship.
9.3 Mandatory tracking and units of measure
Every stock item shall use Serial Number Tracking, Batch Number Tracking, or both Serial and Batch Tracking. No-tracking is not allowed.Manufacturing Date and Expiry Date shall be captured where the approved process requires it to the item.Warranty fields are optional. Warranty duration is configured in Item Master.Item Master shall support Base UOM, Purchase UOM, Sales UOM, conversion factors and barcodes by UOM.
9.4 Transfer workflow
Inventory Transfer Request -> Goods Issue from Source -> In Transit -> Goods Receipt at Destination.The destination warehouse shall confirm actual receipt before the transfer completes.Differences remain in Transit under Investigation.If the missing quantity is found, destination receipt is completed.If it is not found, the Warehouse Manager approves Inventory Loss and the system posts the loss.
9.5 Reservation and availability
Sales Order approval shall reserve stock automatically. The system shall show On Hand, Available, Reserved, In Transit, InQuarantine, Damaged, Returns Stock, Ordered from Suppliers and Committed to Customers by item, warehouse, branchand consolidated company.

9.6 Stock reconciliation
Stock Count Plan -> Physical Count -> Recount where required -> Variance Approval -> Inventory Adjustment.Count scope can be full, by warehouse, item, category or selected filter.The system quantity remains visible during counting.The system calculates physical-to-book differences.Inventory Loss requires Warehouse Manager approval.
9.7 Opening stock
Opening Stock shall be loaded through a dedicated controlled document containing item, branch, warehouse, quantity,UOM, FIFO unit cost, cost-layer date, serial/batch information and applicable manufacture, expiry and warranty data.Approval creates the inventory ledger and opening accounting entry.
9.8 Damaged goods
Damage Report -> Warehouse Manager Approval -> Transfer to Damaged Warehouse -> Inventory Write-Off.Damaged goods cannot be reserved, sold or returned to saleable stock after final damage approval.
9.9 Minimum acceptance criteria
Quantity subledger, FIFO valuation ledger and General Ledger reconcile.No UI, import or API transaction can create negative stock.Serial/batch traceability works from receipt to transfer, delivery, return and write-off.Transfer and count variances remain visible until completed or written off.

11 Logistics Operations
Logistics is a separate revenue service. It manages customer import and shipping service jobs, direct third-party expensesand the separate logistics margin. A logistics job can be linked to the same client import file as a Money Transfer transactionwithout combining their accounting results.
11.1 Module menu and principal screens
Logistics Dashboard; Client Import Files; Service Jobs; Routes and Legs; Carriers; Shipping and Customs Documents;Client Charges; Third-Party Costs; Client Deposits; Job Settlement; Proof of Delivery; Claims and Exceptions; CustomerBilling; Logistics Reports; Logistics Settings.
11.2 Approved workflow
Client Request -> Logistics Job -> Service Charge / Client Funding -> Carrier and Third-Party Execution -> CostRecording -> Delivery Evidence -> Client Settlement / Billing -> Job Close.
11.3 Client-funded import relationship
Goods imported for a client do not enter company warehouses.Goods remain in a financial intermediary account, Client Inventory, until delivery to the client.No Sales Invoice is issued for the goods because the company is providing a service rather than selling the goods.Money Transfer and Logistics are separate services with separate revenue, expenses and margin.Logistics charges paid by the client are added to the client account from the Logistics module.The company does not absorb logistics costs; direct logistics expenses are allocated to the job and deducted fromthe logistics service charge to determine job margin.
11.4 Accounting events
EventDebitCredit

24 Data Architecture, Document Statuses and PostingEngine
This section defines the common technical behaviour that prevents each module from inventing its own status logic,numbering, balances or accounting implementation.
Implementation rule
Module developers shall call shared services for numbering, currency, workflow, posting, attachments and auditlogging. Duplicating these mechanisms inside each module will create inconsistent controls and expensivemaintenance.
Module menu and principal screens
Reference data and configuration administration.Document numbering and fiscal-period sequence management.Posting profiles and account determination rules.Document status and transition configuration.Background jobs and scheduler.Data-quality rules and duplicate detection.Posting preview, posting log and failed-posting queue.Period locks and reprocessing controls.
End-to-end workflow
A business document is created in Draft with header, lines, dimensions, currency and references.Validation checks mandatory fields, master-data status, quantities, amounts, dates, open period, permissions andduplicate conditions.Submission freezes controlled fields and starts the approval workflow.Approval permits the authorised business action; approval itself does not automatically mean accounting postingunless configured.Operational execution records receipt, delivery, service completion, bank movement or other evidence.Posting calls the central engine, resolves accounts/dimensions/rates, generates a balanced journal and subledgerentries within one database transaction.The source document stores posting identifiers and becomes Posted/Completed as applicable.Correction uses authorised cancellation, credit note, return or reversal; the original remains linked and visible.
Core business rules and validations
Header-line architecture is used for documents with multiple items/services; totals are calculated, not manuallystored without reconciliation.Posted journals and subledger entries are append-only; corrections create new linked entries.All money fields store transaction currency amount, base currency amount, currency and rate/reference.Quantities store unit of measure and conversion basis; stock movements use the base inventory unit.All dates distinguish document date, posting date, due date, tax date if required, and system timestamps.Every record includes created-by/at, updated-by/at, status, version and legal entity/branch scope where theapproved process requires it.Soft deletion is limited to eligible draft/configuration records; posted transactions are never deleted.Account determination is configuration-driven using transaction type, item/service group, partner group, warehouse,project and other approved criteria.Posting must be atomic: either all journal/subledger/inventory records commit, or none do.
