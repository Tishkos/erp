## Business dates and money (REQ-HARDEN-001 HARDEN-2)

* "Today" is `businessToday()` from `src/server/domain/business-date.ts` (Asia/Baghdad, `ERP_TIMEZONE`); a timestamp becomes a date with `businessDateOf(instant)`. Never `new Date().toISOString().slice(0, 10)` — it is UTC and reads yesterday for the first three hours of every Baghdad day. `tests/integration/hd07-business-today.test.ts` greps for it and fails the build. SQL that needs today binds `${businessToday()}::date`, never `current_date`.
* Money and quantities are compared and summed as scaled bigints (`parseDecimal` / `parseQuantity` / `divideHalfUp` / `toDecimalString`), never as `Number()`; in the browser the same through `src/lib/decimal.ts`. A `Number()` is only ever the last step before `Intl.NumberFormat`.
* A transition reads its row with `FOR UPDATE` (`lock(tx, id)` in payment-applications, loans, customs-pd, payables; `importOf(…, { lock: true })` in landed-cost) before it checks the status, so a double submit posts once. `tests/integration/hd09-double-submit.test.ts` holds it.

## Audit timestamp payloads

Pass timestamps to `audit.record` as ISO strings, not JavaScript `Date` objects: a Date in an audit before-value was serialized as `{}` by the current audit pipeline. Existing audit rows remain immutable; any correction must be an append-only supplement with verified source evidence.

## Inventory: one ledger, and the scripts that must respect it

`inventory_movement` is the only source of stock quantity. `stock_position`, `positionOf`, the Stock Movement page and the Warehouses Report's Quantity column all sum it; `cost_layer` carries the value. Every document that moves stock writes its rows in the same transaction, so the two cannot drift through the application — only through maintenance.

When a new document table that moves stock is added (migration), add it to **both** `tests/integration/setup.ts` (`resetTestData`) **and** `scripts/ops/format-live-database.sh` (`DOCUMENT_TABLES`). The 2026-09-27 orphans (`TRF-HQ-2026-000001/2`, `ADJ-HQ-2026-000001`) were the format script wiping `inventory_movement` while `stock_transfer`/`stock_adjustment` were missing from its list.

To check a live database: `npx tsx scripts/ops/stock-movement-trace.ts [warehouse] [item]` prints every movement with a running balance and runs `services/inventory-integrity.ts` (documents without ledger rows, rows without documents, unbalanced transfers). To remove a verified orphan document: `scripts/ops/remove-orphan-stock-documents.sh [--yes] TRF-… ADJ-…` — it refuses anything that has movements and writes an `audit_event` per removal.

The Warehouses Report's *Total Price* is IQD at FIFO cost, not a quantity and not a selling price: 507 units bought as 10 @ 50 and 500 @ 500 less 3 sold is 250,350 IQD.

Integration suites: `npx vitest run --project integration tests/integration/ops15-inventory-ledger.test.ts` (≈3 min) covers the lifecycle, warehouse isolation, returns, transfers, shipment stages, concurrent double-posting, negative stock, the integrity checks, invoice reversal, the form-id idempotency key, the warehouse-branch rule, the Stock Ledger and paging. `ops16-document-table-lists.test.ts` derives the document tables from the schema and fails if `format-live-database.sh` or `resetTestData` omits one.

**Two test runs must not share a database.** Another worktree running `test:integration` against `erp_test` at the same time resets it under you — every test fails with "not found" and RLS 42501 errors that look like real bugs. Point `DATABASE_URL_TEST` at your own database (e.g. `erp_test_ledger`; create it as `erp_owner` with `template0`, `lc_collate 'C'`, and `grant connect … to erp_app`).

## Correcting a posted invoice

A posted AP or AR invoice is **reversed** (`ap.reverse` / `ar.reverse`, the Reverse action on its page), never edited: the journal is mirrored through `journal.reverse`, every stock movement is put back through `inventory.reverseMovement` (onto its own FIFO layers), status → `reversed` with `reversal_reason`. Refused while a payment, return, credit memo or payment run rests on it, or while stock from a purchase has been sold or moved on. The reversal is dated the day it is made, so the current period must be open. A Sales/Goods Return is for goods that come back; a reversal is for an invoice that should not have been posted.

## Stock movements carry the warehouse's branch

`inventory.receive/issue/issueFromLayer` refuse a `branchCode` that is not the warehouse's, and migration 0215 holds the same rule by trigger on `inventory_movement` and `cost_layer`. Pass the warehouse's branch (the actor's, after `usableWarehouse`, is the same thing).

## Transfer and Reconciliation forms carry a one-time id

The page mints a UUID into `document_id`; `stock.transfer` / `stock.adjust` take it as the document's id and answer with the existing document on a repeat. New forms that create a stock-moving document on submit should do the same.

## Logistics (REQ-LOG-001)

* **What it is.** The physical journey of imported goods — Import → Shipment (B/L) → Containers → Customs (PD, declaration) → Landed cost → Goods receipt → Inventory — as four headings and fifteen items (`menu.ts` sections `logistics_customs`, `logistics_shipping`, `logistics_in_transit`, `logistics_landed_costs`; the client jobs' section `logistics` is out of the dropdown, Q3). Logistics owns **no money and no stock**: the Import Application, the AP, `inventory_movement`, `cost_layer` and `landed-cost.ts` stay where they are. The old routes keep working (D-FX-1); new screens are `/logistics/…`; only `/inventory/in-transit` redirects (to `/logistics/in-transit`, `next.config.ts`).
* **Reads live in one file.** `services/logistics-views.ts` holds every register the headings show, over the engines' own rows and never recomputing: `dutiesRegister` / `freightRegister` (`landed_cost_charge` by type and state), `documentsRegister` (`attachment` by the record it hangs on — Q6, no category column), `shipmentsRegister` (one row per import with a live B/L), `inTransitInventory`, `transitWarehouses`, `inTransitReconciliation`, `shipmentTracking`, `landedCostsRegister` (the import page's own `lockableState`, said in a word by `domain/import-logistics.lockReason`), `costAllocation` / `locks` (lock → layer adjustment → inventory / COGS → journal), `shippingForImport`, `customsForImport`, `receiptByNo`. Do not add a read to `landed-cost.ts`, `payables.ts` or `inventory.ts` for a Logistics screen.
* **In transit is never on hand.** Stock in a warehouse with `is_transit` or `shipment_stage` is *in transit*: `stock_position` (Inventory's view) counts it in that warehouse's `on_hand` with `available` 0 — `transitWarehouses` reads that row and labels it in transit; `inTransitInventory` reads the posted invoice's cost layers still in transit warehouses (in transit), the container receipts' `moved_qty` (received) and the receipts' own layers (on hand from this import); `inTransitReconciliation` says whether the import rows add up to the ledger per item and reports a difference, never repairs it. 500 shipped, 300 received → in transit 200, received 300, on hand 300 (`tests/integration/log01-chain.test.ts`). `stock_position` itself is Inventory's to change.
* **The customs declaration** (`customs_declaration`, 0301; `services/customs-declarations.ts`, pure rules in `domain/import-logistics.ts`) is what the broker lodged against a PD: declared value, duty, taxes, fees, HS code, in its own currency — **declared figures only**, frozen once it leaves draft (trigger `customs_declaration_frozen`), never posted, never a charge. Draft → Submitted → Accepted (needs `customs_ref`) → Assessed → Cleared; Cancelled / Rejected with a reason; forward one step at a time; the three ends are final; never deleted. `create` / `edit_draft` / `submit` with the PD's grants, `reverse_cancel` (cancel, reject) with the PD's `import` grant. The record shows declared beside charged (`chargedFor`: the import's live `customs_asycuda` charges and how much a lock has taken); a difference is information. Series `CUSTOMS_DECLARATION` (CDN-{BRANCH}-{YYYY}-{SERIAL}), events `DECLARATION_RAISED` / `DECLARATION_MOVED` / `DECLARATION_CLEARED` in the PD lane; in `resetTestData` and `format-live-database.sh`.

## Inventory — the landed-cost engine, Final Cost, valuation and the screens (IMPROVEMENT-002, 2026-10-04)

* **One source of stock truth stays the movement ledger and its FIFO layers.** The import's landed cost decides what stock finally cost; Projects/WBS meet it only through the movement. Nothing in `landed-cost.ts`, `inventory-departures.ts` or `final-cost.ts` imports sales, projects or purchasing: each module answers for its own movement kinds in `src/server/departures/{sales,projects,purchasing,stock}.ts`, composed by `departures-runtime.ts` (called from `registerAllRecords()`; a lock in a cold process would otherwise refuse every departure).
* **A lock** (`landed-cost.lock`) locks the import (`FOR UPDATE`), then the layers by item and warehouse in `inventory.issue`'s order, follows every received layer's stock by fact (`cost_layer.origin_layer_id`; older data by pairing, refused when ambiguous or still in transit), nets each consuming movement of its reversal and of what a sales return brought back before the lock, restates what is on hand, and sends the departed share where the business event took it — `cogs` with the sale's own line and dimensions, `project_material_cost` with the issue's, `inventory_loss`, `purchase_variance` / `return_clearing` (the goods return's `reimburses_landed_cost`), `inventory_adjustment` — one journal line per destination and one `landed_cost_departure` row per lock × layer × movement (0285: append-only, the deferred trigger holds Σ departure + rounding = `lock.cogs_iqd`). Never "everything that left is COGS": a kind nobody answers for refuses the lock naming it. Lock dates: not in the future, not before the previous lock, not before the last container receipt.
* **Stock that comes back after a lock brings its share back** (`inventory-departures.returnLandedShare`): Dr the item's inventory / Cr the departure's account and dimensions, dated the day it came back, one negative `return` row per departure it undoes (0285's trigger: never more than left). Called by `inventory.reverseMovement` (the layer already carries the restated cost; the return is exactly what the layer's value rose beyond the restored cost), `sales-return.accept` (the return layer is raised to the restated cost) and `project-execution.postIssue` for a return line naming its issue line (`returns_issue_line_id`, 0286; the project's negative cost row follows through the handler's `returned` hook). A later lock reaches returned units on their return layers (`cameBack`).
* **A settled project is never reopened**: the share goes to the issue's receiver flagged `post_settlement`, and `project_settlement_supplement` (0286) carries it on — an asset settlement posts Dr AUC / Cr the cost under `projects.settlement`; a result settlement nothing more. No `project_cost` row is written on a closed project (the database refuses it).
* **Stock Count** posts `inventory_adjustment` through `inventory.stock_adjustment` (a surplus Dr inventory / Cr adjustment, valued at `unitCostForFound`); `inventory_loss` is for write-off and damage only. **Decided by default, owner to confirm:** `inventory_loss` stays unmapped in `POSTING_MAP` — a lock with a write-off departure refuses and names the role; reimbursement = Yes leaves the landed part in `return_clearing` until payables' standalone credit note clears it.
* **Final Cost** is `services/final-cost.ts` (the import page reads it; `landed-cost.finalCost` is superseded): per received layer purchase → each lock → final over the whole lineage (A1 a sold-out layer keeps its charges, A2 moved stock is on hand, A3 the value is remaining × cost, A11 half up), the agreed currency derived from `ap_invoice.agreed_currency/rate`, departures grouped by transaction type with document numbers (never a project code), each lock reconciled. It prints as `landed_cost_final` (`print/landed-cost-documents.ts`), offered under the import page's Final item cost table.
* **Valuation at a date** is `services/inventory-valuation.ts`: quantity from the ledger at the date, unit cost today less the locks dated after it, each layer rounded once; `final` through the unbroken run of closed periods, `provisional` after, a future date refused, an issue dated before its layer's receipt flagged and never corrected; per inventory account against the posted balance at the date. `stockAgainstGl` is what `closing-checks.ts` compares (exact, at the period's end). `periods` is imported lazily there so the close-check catalogue unit test stays database-free.
* **`stock_position`** (0287): a transit warehouse's goods are `in_transit`, not `on_hand`; `held` is the physical quantity `positionOf` and the negative-stock rule read. The item master's stock-by-warehouse list reads `held`.
* **Screens** (the twelve on the Inventory menu; Items, Units and Warehouses live under Master Data): Stock Count (`/inventory/stock-count` + `[countNo]` on `services/stock-count.ts` — plan, count on the lines, recount, approve, adjust with a department, cancel; four doors from the plan; print `stock_count`, grants in 0288), Stock Receipts and Stock Issues (`services/stock-flows.ts`, read-only over the ledger, every row opens its document), Availability (on order from approved purchase orders, unit cost and value from the layers, category and branch filters, each row opens its ledger), Inventory Valuation (`/inventory/valuation`, print `inventory_valuation`), Warehouses Report (a summary per warehouse — items, units, value, low stock by `item.min_stock_level` (0288), negative stock, last movement — drilling down warehouse → items → movements), Inventory Checks (`/inventory/checks`, `services/inventory-checks.ts`: twelve read-only checks, healthy / warning / error, never repairs; print `inventory_checks`). The Opening Stock record and the item and warehouse profiles wear the shared doors; inventory's attachment parents are registered in `attachments-runtime.ts`.
* **Tests:** the gate is `lc01-landed-cost-routing` (every scenario ends on `support/landed-cost-balance.ts`: remaining + departed = the charge, layers = G/L), `lc02-goods-return-landed`, `lc03-final-cost`, `ap07-landed-cost`, `ops15-inventory-ledger`, `pm03-execution` and the unit `lc-final-cost`; the screens `tests/e2e/inventory-screens.spec.ts`.
